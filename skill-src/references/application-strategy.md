Which roles to prioritize and why — grounded in the user's actual profile strength (from
market research / CV diagnosis), not just stated preference.

## Company Strategy

Suggest the types of companies worth targeting based on the user's profile and goals:
Startups, Scaleups, Consulting firms, Banks, Insurance companies, Universities, Research labs,
SaaS companies, AI product companies, Public sector organizations, Healthcare tech companies,
EdTech companies, FinTech companies.

Different company types have different hiring bars and speeds — e.g. startups often hire
faster with less rigid requirements but higher risk; banks/insurance tend to have stricter
ATS filtering and slower processes but more stability. Make this trade-off explicit rather
than just listing categories.

## Application Quality Tiers

Split applications into three tiers:

### High Priority
Fully tailored CV and cover letter. Reserve for roles that are strong fits or high-interest
targets — this takes real time per application, so it shouldn't be diluted across everything.

### Medium Priority
Lightly tailored CV only (reordered/adjusted bullets, no full rewrite, no cover letter unless
required).

### Low Priority
Quick-apply only — existing CV, no customization. Use for volume/optionality, not as the
primary strategy.
